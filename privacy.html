Privacy Policy — RoleFit Studio

Last updated: November 2026

RoleFit Studio is an open-source, privacy-first ATS resume and job-match workspace maintained at github.com/zwhy149/rolefit-studio.

1. Standalone Web Mode

When used directly in a web browser outside Anna AI OS, all resume parsing, ATS skill-gap scoring, and bullet diagnostics execute 100% locally in your browser. Saved drafts are stored only in your browser's localStorage and are never transmitted to any developer-operated server.

2. Anna AI OS App Mode

When installed inside Anna AI OS, RoleFit Studio uses Anna's official Host APIs on your behalf:

  • anna.storage.* (Anna Persistent Storage, app-scoped) to save your master resume and tracked job analyses under your own Anna account.
  • anna.llm.complete to generate grounded bullet rewrites, hiring-bar summaries, outreach notes, and interview prep using your chosen Anna model.
  • anna.web.search and anna.web.fetch only when you explicitly fetch a job URL or run Live Company Intel.

The developer of RoleFit Studio operates no external telemetry, analytics, ad trackers, or third-party databases.

3. Contact

Questions or issues can be reported via GitHub Issues.
